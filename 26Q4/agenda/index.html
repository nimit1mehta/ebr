ContextWhere we fit
  • Every camp moved toward context in the last six months. Most of the new launches infer context from the data. We provide authoritative context, governed and approved by the business.
  • We win with large enterprises that need governed context across many systems and have a deadline.
  • We lose when buyers want fast, simple or code-first tools.

For decisionRecommendation

Prepare for a sale and launch in Q1 2027.

What this means
  • Interview bankers in November.
  • Canvas known relationships for term sheets.
  • Address the key gaps in the business through the 2027 plan:
    • Product story: tighten and test it to drive new logos.
    • Delivery: redesign around forward-deployed engineering, supported by the India office.
    • Architecture: run an architecture review so the technology meets acquisition diligence.
    • Partners: engage and enable the SIs we know, such as Accenture, to support implementation and growth.
Why this approach
  • From strength: year end ahead of the board plan, retention ahead of plan, and expansion in our core accounts.
  • Competitive pressure: every major platform is moving into context, mostly inferred, and consolidation is underway. The window to sell on authoritative context is now.
  • Timing: we close the year with Lockheed and J&J, and we expect to be included in the Gartner Magic Quadrant in November.
Caveats
  • We watch two things: the multiples buyers are paying, and our growth rate.
  • If multiples come in below expectations, or our growth outpaces the plan, we reassess in December.
Questions for discussion
  • Banker-led or relationship-led? Known relationships matter, and the bankers we have met have not impressed us on generating demand.
  • What floor would make us walk away, and how do we set it before we hear from buyers?
  • How do we handle concentration in diligence: Lockheed, J&J and the Progress OEM relationship, including change of control?
