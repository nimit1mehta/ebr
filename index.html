Methodology and sources
Projection
  • Retention by risk (open accounts): Low 100%, Medium 90%, High 75%, applied to the account's 2025 ARR. Risk is the renewal Risk level.
  • Projected 2026 ARR: booked renewals use the Master file Bookings actual (ARR Segment, finance-invoiced); open renewals use the unweighted CSM forecast × retention(risk). This changes PROJECTED ARR only - ACTUAL and RETAINED are unchanged.
Formulas
  • GRR (P) [company]: (book gross retained + multi-year-locked 2025 ARR + partner ARR, all retained, capped at prior, no uplift) divided by the company 2025 software base (book + locked + partner). This is the company % gross renewed - the headline GRR.
  • % Gross Renewed (book): book gross retained divided by the book 2025 ARR - a book-subset retention rate, not the company GRR.
  • NRR (P): (sum of 2026 actual-or-projected over the book + multi-year-locked 2026 ARR + partner ARR + bookings upsell & expansion) divided by the 2025 base (book 2025 ARR + locked 2025 ARR + partner). NRR captures uplift/upsell; GRR does not.

  • The site measures no conversions at all. Zero key events across every month read (Apr–Aug). Every funnel stage below Sessions is unmeasurable, and the website cannot be credited or blamed for a single SAL. Fixing this is worth more than any traffic number on this page. Owner: Ingrid.
  • Unassigned is now the largest channel — 43.5% of Q3 TD sessions. It was 0.7% in April and 31.9% by June. GA4’s own insight fingers 27 Jul, when unassigned spiked to 878 on outbound/BDR links to /topquadrant-webinar-yaml-demo-day. Outbound links are being tagged with UTMs GA4 cannot map to a channel group, so nearly half of traffic is attributionally dark and the channel section understates outbound.
  • April’s 8,476 sessions are not real demand. 7,103 of them (83.8%) are Referral at 0s average engagement — the signature of bot or redirect traffic, not humans. Q2 traffic should not be quoted at 12,123 sessions; ex-April-referral it is closer to 5,000.
  • Over half of trailing-12-month traffic is three spikes that do not look human. Nov 25 Direct 3,171 at 5.0% engagement and 3s; Feb 26 Referral 9,305 at 0s average engagement; Apr 26 Referral 7,103 at 0s. Together 19,579 of 36,048 sessions — 54%. Strip them and the site runs at roughly 600–900 real sessions a month, flat, all year. Any year-on-year traffic claim built on the raw number is wrong.
  • Organic search is effectively zero — 3, 9, 55, 46 and 36 sessions in Apr–Aug, 2.4% of Q3 TD. For a category people actively search, the site is invisible. Email at 33.6% is carrying the channel mix almost alone.
