Projection

Formulas
  • GRR (P) [company]: (book gross retained + multi-year-locked 2025 ARR + partner ARR, all retained, capped at prior, no uplift) divided by the company 2025 software base (book + locked + partner). This is the company % gross renewed - the headline GRR.
  • % Gross Renewed (book): book gross retained divided by the book 2025 ARR - a book-subset retention rate, not the company GRR.
  • NRR (P): (sum of 2026 actual-or-projected over the book + multi-year-locked 2026 ARR + partner ARR + bookings upsell & expansion) divided by the 2025 base (book 2025 ARR + locked 2025 ARR + partner). NRR captures uplift/upsell; GRR does not.

Board update · Q3 2026

Executive Summary

State of the Business

  • Expansion / upsell continue to carry bookings, driven by the SaaS/TQDF conversion process and customers’ move toward AI-ready data foundations. To and through the product launch, customers have predictably (Spotify, Expedia) and unpredictably (Mars Vet, Paramount/Pluto TV) converted.
  • New-logo closes continue to lag — top-of-funnel and net-new conversion remain the constraint. Buyer maturity and clarification on differentiation has taken more time than expected.
  • Demand: to address, demand focus has been high — webinars, nurture, and intent-based outbound stood up with ZRev; strongest pipeline-generation month YTD in June.
  • Churn: on track for churn numbers this year with 4 of 8 signal churn accounts able to be won back — 95% GRR if we run the table. Key logos:
    • Capital One — vibe-coded an alternative but are indicating they are not going to churn.
    • NBCU and Amgen — active deals to win them back with positive Q3 targets.
    • Elsevier — renewal revived and quote in hand.
  • Debt refinanced and $1M raised; cash dips but is modeled sustainable (~$400K trough in November).
Board topics

Refinancing Debt

  • Refinanced the outstanding facility and raised $1M from investors. Terms: 12-month term, 6-month guarantee, 17% rate.
  • Cash will dip but should be sustainable — trough of ~$400K in November. Cash-flow model to be actively managed through the period.
  • Appreciate the support from Piyush in getting this done.

Demand Generation

  • Demand was the most important workstream for the quarter — standardized plays (webinars, email nurture, intent-based outbound, conferences) now run on a repeatable cadence with ZRev, and a campaign playbook is standing the motion up as automated and repeatable. Lessons:
    • Webinars were under-used and are higher engagement for converting to sales conversations — moving to 2–3/month (Q3 plan = 8).
    • Nurture off the existing database is the highest-ROI motion (1→5 in Q3).
    • Intent-based outbound + ABM scaling with ZRev (1→7 in Q3).
  • Demand motion is early: the ZRev-run plays (HCLS webinar, FAIR Data for Pharma nurture) are standing up but have not yet produced attributable pipeline or wins; the motion needs more cycles before it can be credited.

Website & Digital Presence

  • The new website launches 8/2 — a full rebuild of structure, design, and GTM messaging, driven by TQ.
  • It sets the stage for new plays. With the site live, ZRev can stand up paid-ads campaigns against a modern, conversion-ready destination, and we can bring our own AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) best practices to bear so TQ surfaces well in AI-driven search and answer engines.
  • Sequencing: the broader AI-positioning campaign work is on hold until Fall, deliberately sequenced to land behind the relaunch.

Rest of the Year Outlook

  • The plan for H2 rests on two things: generating enough pipeline to hit the demand plan, and closing the bookings already in front of us. This section is how we get there.
  • Demand — hitting the pipeline-generation plan. $3M budget / $4M target of new pipeline for the next two quarters, having succeeded in generating ~$2M in Q2.
    • The demand engine will keep a steady cadence of known plays (webinars, email nurture, intent-based outbound, conferences).
    • The new webpage will prioritize directing folks to our home page via paid and founder-led social media to produce against that number month by month.
  • Bookings — Q3+Q4 bookings number is $1.1M, split roughly evenly for each quarter.
    • Q3: target a $600K quarter with $365K of New Logo forecast (Healthstream, Vanguard, Qnity, and Versant) + Upsell + Expansion $410K (Central Arizona Project, BMS, Amgen).
    • Q4: deals on the horizon with true momentum — Merck, Lockheed, and J&J expansion.
    • Full $3M of pipeline to work while these deals are processed.

Smartlogic

  • Smartlogic is a ~$600K white-label partner currently undergoing an architectural review.
  • Key risk: they write us out of the platform. Things that need to be true, and mitigants:
    • We aren’t able to advocate for our position — Actions: multi-threaded approach bringing PM, dev lead, chief architect, and exec to connect across the company.
    • We don’t accelerate their delivery of features — Actions: working sessions and forced interaction with their tech architects, mandated by their leadership.
    • We’re not investing / meeting them halfway — Actions: verbal commitment of pipeline and a request for product proposal from the PM involved.
  • Scheduling an in-person brainstorming session with their chief architect and have committed to them that we are looking to support.
  • Reaching out to their CEO and looking to set up a regular cadence with him in the coming quarter.
  • Key opportunity: getting them on TQ Data Foundation as a white-label with improved economics and revenue predictability.

Sale Process & Timeline

  • Where we left off (last board meeting): two goals set for this workstream — improve the business profile and manage the banker process. This quarter’s focus was the business profile. We made good improvements; the job now is to keep our head down and finish the work (see below).
  • Quarterly strategy: have a strong quarter and engage bankers to make a selection in mid-October.
    • Execute the current playbook for cost management and hitting growth, demand, and AI numbers.
    • Re-engage the bankers previously discussed with a profile that supports the conversation.
    • Begin the process of banker cultivation and pitches in September.
