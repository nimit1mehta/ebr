Progress / Semaphore

  • Relationship. Progress white-labels TopBraid Live (TBL) inside Semaphore. Historically we overlapped in market, with Semaphore focused on tagging and unstructured data.
  • Why it matters now. Semaphore is the semantic layer of the Progress Data Platform, so its scale is now tied to Progress's AI strategy.
  • Root cause. Progress sees hours-long restarts, slowdowns as models and users grow, and no path past one server. All of it traces to TBL holding Semaphore's working state in memory on a single server, on an embedded TBL build about 2 years old.
  • In flight. Option A, a joint proof of concept, reads out in December. Moving change history out of the graph should cut startup 50-80% and fit 2-5x the data on the same server. It still leaves one server.
  • Our recommendation. Redesigned horizontal scaling (Option C) is the only path that removes the single-server limit. It is 12-18 months, phased, earliest Q1 2028, and needs a joint development commitment.
  • Q4 asks of Progress. An executive sponsor and a read on how central this is to their strategy, the workload profile and a timeline owner to close out Option A, and a technical decision maker for Options B and C.

                              Semaphore runs on our TopBraid Live, and fixing its scale limit is now on the critical path for the Progress Data Platform. The December proof-of-concept readout and three Q4 decisions from Progress set the next step.

                              Executive summary
                              • Relationship. Progress white-labels TopBraid Live (TBL) inside Semaphore. Historically we overlapped in market, with Semaphore focused on tagging and unstructured data.
                              • Why it matters now. Semaphore is the semantic layer of the Progress Data Platform, so its scale is now tied to Progress's AI strategy.
                              • Root cause. Progress sees hours-long restarts, slowdowns as models and users grow, and no path past one server. All of it traces to TBL holding Semaphore's working state in memory on a single server, on an embedded TBL build about 2 years old.

                              • Q4 asks of Progress. An executive sponsor and a read on how central this is to their strategy, the workload profile and a timeline owner to close out Option A, and a technical decision maker for Options B and C.

                                  • Next release. 9.4.0 targets November 2026 and is 47% through development. Releases ship roughly every three months.
                                  • Headline feature. A single conversational Copilot inside the workspace turns natural-language requests into live queries, edits and answers, replacing today's scattered AI tools.
